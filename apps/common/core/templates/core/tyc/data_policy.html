{% blocktrans trimmed %} PROPENSIONES ABOGADOS, identified with NIT [pending], with its main address in [city] (hereinafter, "Propensiones"), operator of the GEA platform, has adopted this personal data processing policy in compliance with the constitutional and legal provisions in force, recognising the importance of guaranteeing the security, privacy and confidentiality of the personal data of its clients, users, employees, suppliers, partners and every other group whose information it processes. {% endblocktrans %}

3. {% trans 'Purpose of this policy' %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} To protect the constitutional right of habeas data, guaranteeing that the people whose data we hold can know it, update it and correct it. Propensiones only stores and processes data that has been authorised by its owner, and applies the security measures needed to protect it. {% endblocktrans %}

7. {% trans 'Authorisation' %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Propensiones requests the prior, express and informed consent of the data owner. That authorisation is recorded, together with when it was given, so that it can be produced later as article 9 of Law 1581 requires. Authorisation is not needed where the data is requested by a public body exercising its legal functions, where the data is public, in cases of medical urgency, for authorised historical or statistical purposes, or in relation to the civil registry. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans 'Sensitive data and data of minors' %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Where sensitive data is involved, the owner is told that they are not obliged to authorise it, which data is sensitive, and what it will be used for. The platform is not intended for minors and does not knowingly collect their data; where it were necessary, it would be handled through their legal representative and after hearing the minor, as articles 6 and 12 of Decree 1377 of 2013 require. {% endblocktrans %}

9. {% trans 'How long we keep it' %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Data is processed while the relationship with the owner lasts, plus the period required by the applicable law. Two categories last longer, for a reason worth stating plainly: {% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Certified documents and their fingerprints. A certificate whose record is deleted stops being verifiable, which defeats the point of having issued it. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} The audit trail. The record of who did what is what allows the firm to show it acted correctly, including towards the person asking. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Beyond the general commitment, these are the specific measures in place, so that the statement can be checked rather than taken on trust: {% endblocktrans %}

  • {% trans 'Identity data — passport number, date of birth, nationality, address and telephone — is stored encrypted, not in readable form.' %}
  • {% trans 'Certificate document numbers are never stored in the clear: only a keyed hash of them is kept.' %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Propensiones assumes the duties of a controller under article 17 of Law 1581, and, where it processes data on behalf of a third party, those of a processor under article 18. Where both roles coincide, both sets of duties apply. {% endblocktrans %}

17. {% trans 'Automatic translation' %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Some texts in the platform are translated automatically between Spanish and English. That translation is machine-generated: it is a convenience, not a certified translation, and it is not reviewed by a person before being shown. Where a text has legal or contractual effect, the version written by its author is the one that governs. {% endblocktrans %}
